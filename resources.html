Mental Health Resources & Tools

Access evidence-based clinical screening assessments and immediate psychological support helplines.

DASS-21 Test

The Depression Anxiety Stress Scales is a 21-item self-report questionnaire designed to measure and monitor emotional distress states.

The Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS) is an initial screening symptom checklist to map out tendencies related to attention and hyperactivity.
